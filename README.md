# Laser link demo: PolSK-4 vs ASK-4

Pick a picture (or use your own photo) and send it through our free-space optical link, then compare what arrives with
polarisation shift keying (PolSK-4) and amplitude shift keying (ASK-4).

Every received pixel comes from receiver decisions recorded on the lab bench (147 matched PolSK-4 / ASK-4 recordings at
10 MBd through emulated turbulence). Each transmission replays one recording at the chosen strength from a random starting
point. Everything runs in the browser: uploaded photos are processed on the visitor's device and never uploaded.

Wits University, Optical Communication Lab. Project 26P83 / 26G21: *Performance Analysis of Polarisation Shift Keying in
Free Space Optical Communication*. Reinhard Jansen van Vuuren and Aatikah Sheikh; supervisor Prof. Mitchell A. Cox.
